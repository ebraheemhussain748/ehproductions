Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Ebraheem Hussain Productions is a design, video, printing and web studio based in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. For any privacy question or request, write to ebraheemhussain748@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 93890 80059.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number or email, the category you choose, an optional budget and your message. We use these only to reply to you and prepare a quote.
  • Reviews: your name, an optional business name, your rating and your review. A review is shown publicly on this website only after we approve it.
  • WhatsApp buttons: they open WhatsApp with a ready-made message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and what you send is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.
  • Your quote list and currency choice in the catalog are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there when you come back. They are never sent to us unless you send the list yourself.

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or government ID numbers on this website.

Cookies

This website does not set tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner. A YouTube video loads only after you press play, from youtube-nocookie.com, and YouTube's own policy applies to that video.

Services that handle your data

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): stores enquiries and reviews. Google's servers for this project may be located outside India.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): sends a copy of each enquiry to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and, like any web host, may log your IP address for security.
  • Google (gstatic.com): serves the Firebase code the forms need.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com): serves the sample product photos. Your browser asks Unsplash for each photo, so Unsplash sees your IP address, as with any website image.

We share your details with no one else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years after our last conversation, so we can follow up on your project and keep a record of our work. Approved reviews stay on the website until you ask us to remove them. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it or delete it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • raise a complaint.

Email ebraheemhussain748@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Security

The website is served only over HTTPS. Enquiries can be read only from our admin account, which needs a Google sign-in. Database rules block everyone else from reading them. No system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information through the form.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
